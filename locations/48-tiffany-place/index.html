A small, detached home on cobblestone Tiffany Place in Brooklyn’s Columbia Street Waterfront District. The property’s compact footprint offers a different pattern of living from a continuous row of street-front houses.

Background

An archived listing records a 2013 renovation with Massim Studio. The design used double-height space, exposed structural steel and high windows to bring light into the house. It is a private residence, included here for its arrangement and scale.
