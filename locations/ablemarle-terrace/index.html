Albemarle Terrace

Directions ↗Street View ↗

A residential court off East 21st Street in Flatbush, lined by a unified group of early twentieth-century row houses. It forms a small historic district together with nearby Kenmore Terrace.

Background

The houses were designed by Slee & Bryson. The city's designation report describes Albemarle's neo-Federal architecture and the coordinated scale and materials that make the court feel like a distinct piece of the neighborhood.
