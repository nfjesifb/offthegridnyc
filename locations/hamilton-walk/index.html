Hamilton Walk

Directions ↗Street View ↗

A pedestrian residential lane off 94th Street between Third and Fourth avenues in Bay Ridge. Low brick houses and small gardens face a shared walkway.

Background

Hamilton Walk runs parallel to nearby Lafayette Walk. The two lanes make a useful comparison: each fits a small residential community into the interior of a large block, using a footpath instead of a traffic street.
