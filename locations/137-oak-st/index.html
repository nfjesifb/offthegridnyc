137 Oak St

Directions ↗Street View ↗

The former Greenpoint Home for the Aged at Oak and Guernsey Streets. The building occupies the bend in the street, giving it a distinctive setting within Greenpoint’s historic district.

Background

The Landmarks Preservation Commission identifies it as an 1886–87 design by Theobald Engelhardt, whose work also includes nearby houses and St. John’s Evangelical Lutheran Church. Its institutional history adds context to a building that can otherwise look like an unusually large house. Current interior access is not offered by this guide.
