Greenwich Mews

Directions ↗Street View ↗

A group of townhouses arranged as a residential mews at 687 Greenwich Street, south of West 10th Street. The inward-facing layout is a comparatively recent addition to the Village's older streets.

Background

Village Preservation dates the development to 1986 and records that it replaced a garage. This is an example of the courtyard form being reused in modern housing, rather than simply surviving from the carriage-house era.
