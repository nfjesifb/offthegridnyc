Unusual homes

5 Carmine St ABC

Directions ↗Street View ↗

A cluster of homes within the block behind 5 Carmine Street in Greenwich Village. A brick-walled passage and gated courtyard separate this small residential interior from the busy street frontage.

Background

The city’s historic-district report describes the passage to the interior houses and traces the street-front complex to buildings constructed around 1829 and 1832. Later alterations joined and changed the frontage. The distinction between the commercial street edge and the homes behind it is what makes this address unusual.
