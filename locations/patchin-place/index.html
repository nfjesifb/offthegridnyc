Patchin Place

Satellite view

About

A small, gated alley in Greenwich Village, lined with historic townhouses.

History

Patchin Place was developed in the 1840s and has been home to various artists and writers, including E.E. Cummings and Djuna Barnes. The alley's quaint atmosphere and historic architecture make it a unique part of the Village's cultural landscape.
