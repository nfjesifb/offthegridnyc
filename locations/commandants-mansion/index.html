Commandants Mansion

Directions ↗Street View ↗

The former home of the Brooklyn Navy Yard commandant, also called Quarters A. This Federal-style house sits on a rise by Evans Street in Vinegar Hill, apart from the surrounding street grid.

Background

The Navy dates its completion to 1806, when Jonathan Thorn became the yard’s first commandant. It later became a National Historic Landmark. The house is privately owned; its presence in the neighborhood can be appreciated from public streets.
