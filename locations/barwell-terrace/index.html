Barwell Terrace

Directions ↗Street View ↗

A narrow residential court in Bay Ridge, between Third and Fourth avenues around 97th–98th streets. Houses face an interior pedestrian space rather than a conventional traffic street.

Background

The city's Chester Court designation report identifies Barwell Terrace alongside Hamilton Walk and Lafayette Walk as examples of houses clustered around narrow courts closed to vehicles. These are lived-in spaces; view from the entrance unless access is clearly permitted.
