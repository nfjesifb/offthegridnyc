Kenmore Terrace

Directions ↗Street View ↗

A small residential court in Flatbush, paired with neighboring Albemarle Terrace in a designated historic district. Its low houses form a coherent enclosure around the street.

Background

Designed by Slee & Bryson, Kenmore shows the influence of the Garden City movement. The landmark report notes the houses' incorporated garages as a sign of changing domestic life and the growing role of the automobile.
