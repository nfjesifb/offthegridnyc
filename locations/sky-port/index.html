Skyport Seaplane Terminal

Directions ↗Street View ↗

A seaplane terminal at the East River end of East 23rd Street. Aircraft use the water alongside the marina, placing a small aviation facility within Manhattan’s waterfront infrastructure.

Background

DockNYC identifies the site as Skyport’s seaplane terminal, beside Skyport Marina. The city’s lease history for this waterfront property extends back to 1959 and includes marina and seaplane uses. This is an operating transport facility; check the operator for any travel arrangements.
