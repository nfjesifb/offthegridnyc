Lafayette Walk

Directions ↗Street View ↗

A short pedestrian-only residential lane entered from 94th Street in Bay Ridge. Brick row houses and planted front gardens line the route, parallel to Hamilton Walk.

Background

The walk is less than a block long. Its interest lies in how the houses address a narrow shared space rather than the surrounding avenues. Compare the two neighboring walks from their street entrances and respect residential access.
