Jones Wood Garden

Directions ↗Street View ↗

A shared garden inside an Upper East Side block, enclosed by twelve townhouses on East 65th and East 66th Streets. Separate backyards were brought together to create a larger common landscape.

Background

The Smithsonian’s garden archive traces the scheme to architect Edwin S. Hewitt’s purchase of the twelve brownstones in 1919. He removed dividing fences and walls and introduced paths, planting beds and water features. Covenants protected the shared arrangement. This is a private residents’ garden.
