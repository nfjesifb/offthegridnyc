Chester Court

Directions ↗Street View ↗

A short street off Flatbush Avenue lined by a coordinated group of Tudor Revival row houses. Repeated gables, brickwork and half-timbered upper stories give the court a distinct character.

Background

The development was planned by Peter J. Collins in 1911–12, drawing on the architecture he had seen in Chester, England. The Landmarks Preservation Commission's designation report explains how this suburban-looking form was fitted into an urban row-house development.
