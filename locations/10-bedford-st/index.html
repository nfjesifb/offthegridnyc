Unusual homes

10 Bedford St

Directions ↗Street View ↗

A separate backhouse behind the street-facing building at 10 Bedford Street. It is a small example of the second layer of homes concealed inside many Greenwich Village blocks.

Background

Village Preservation documents how the backhouse was visible from Downing Street while an adjoining lot stood empty. Construction on that lot in 2009 concealed it again. The interesting feature is the relationship between front house, courtyard and rear dwelling, rather than a public entrance from the street.
