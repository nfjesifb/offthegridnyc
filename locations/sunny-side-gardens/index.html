Sunnyside Gardens

Directions ↗Street View ↗

Low-rise homes arranged around shared interior gardens in Sunnyside, Queens. Paths between the houses connect a network of courts, giving the block interiors a different character from the surrounding streets.

Background

Built mainly from 1924 to 1928, Sunnyside Gardens was designed by Clarence Stein, Henry Wright, Frederick Ackerman and landscape architect Marjorie Cautley. Its small private yards and larger common gardens made open space part of the housing plan. The neighborhood became a New York City historic district in 2007; the shared gardens remain residential spaces.
