Rooftop Cape Cod

Directions ↗Street View ↗

A shingled cottage on top of 72 East 1st Street, at First Avenue. Its pitched roof and terrace place the silhouette of a small coastal house above an East Village building.

Background

The cottage belongs to a private penthouse and has been described in property records as a separate studio above the main duplex. It is an example of rooftop space taking on the form of a house and yard. There is no public rooftop access.
