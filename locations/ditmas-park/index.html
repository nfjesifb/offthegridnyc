A neighborhood in Brooklyn known for its large, Victorian-style homes and tree-lined streets.

Background

Ditmas Park was developed in the early 20th century as a suburban enclave within Brooklyn. The neighborhood's homes, many of which are freestanding with front porches and gardens, were designed to attract middle-class families seeking a respite from urban density.
