Why

The political economy of niceness

New York is loud, dense and relentless, and yet it is full of mews, courtyards, shared gardens, rooftop tracks and waterfront edges where the city goes quiet. Most were built by a specific mix of money, zoning and stubborn neighbors. This map collects them.

Every location was visited or vetted by a person. The write-ups are drafted with AI and edited by hand, so treat them as a starting point, not gospel. Some places are private, gated or only open at certain hours: look before you go, and be a good guest when you get there.
